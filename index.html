MEET YOUR CHIROPRACTOR

Local roots.
A personal connection.

Meet Rowan Miller.

Raised on a sheep and beef farm on the west coast of Golden Bay, Rowan understands the people and rhythms of rural life.

He was Senior Scholar in physiology at Auckland University in 1998 and the top academic student at the New Zealand College of Chiropractic in 2001, following five years of study.

After providing rural chiropractic care on the West Coast, Rowan opened his Motueka practice in 2003 and Takaka in 2005. Today, he works part-time in both communities alongside other interests, including farming.

Rowan and Drea have four children and are involved in the leadership team at Kahurangi Christian Church.
